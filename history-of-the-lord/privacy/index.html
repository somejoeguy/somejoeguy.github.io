Privacy Policy for History of the Lord

Last updated: November 8, 2026

History of the Lord ("the app") is a Bible reading, biblical history and gratitude journal app published by Honor Bound Assets. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your gratitude journal, reading position and recently viewed entries stay on your phone. We do not collect, see or store them.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Information that stays on your device

The following is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone and is never sent to us:

  • Gratitude journal entries you write
  • The last Bible chapter you opened and the history entries you viewed recently

If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include this data in your device backup to your own Google account, under Google's terms. We cannot access those backups. Uninstalling the app deletes this data from your phone.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner and full-screen ads on some screens. Ads never appear while you read scripture or write in your gratitude journal.

To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Your device's advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Information about your interactions with ads, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as app crashes and performance

We do not receive information that identifies you personally. We limit ads to content rated suitable for general audiences.

You can control personalised advertising:

  • In the app: if you are in a region where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland), the app asks for your choice when first opened. You can change it any time with "Ad privacy choices" at the bottom of the Home screen.
  • On your device: go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Permissions

The app requests internet access only to load ads. Scripture, history content and your journal work fully offline.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
